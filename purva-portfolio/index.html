Chapter 1 My story

A data engineer who packed her bags 🧳

I studied Computer Science in Mandsaur and started my career in 2020 in Udaipur, writing SQL and keeping data pipelines healthy. Next I moved to Pune, where Capgemini brought me into big banking programmes for Citi Bank and Citizens Bank: migrations to Snowflake, dbt, Airflow and cloud data on GCP.

Then came the big move. I landed in Stockholm, where I helped build Swedbank's lakehouse on Azure Databricks. Today I live in Kista, I'm learning Swedish (and swimming), and I'm building AI apps and agents through Microsoft's AI Aspire programme.

What stayed the same in every city: I love automating the boring parts so people can trust their data.
